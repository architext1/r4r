Seven ways to deform

If every healthy system settles at 1/φ, then every chronic stress pattern is a specific way of being pulled off it. Addendum A names seven, gives each one a shape, and notes — without making anything of it for a paragraph or two — that they are the seven deadly sins.

§A1 · Overview

Not a moral claim

The correspondence is structural, not moral, and the addendum is explicit about it: each "sin" describes a failure mode of the five-bus architecture, not a character deficiency. What the old vocabulary supplies is a thousand years of careful observation about how people come apart, already sorted into categories. What the geometry supplies is a way to discuss those categories without the moral loading.

The seven geometries are the seven ways a system can deform when it runs alone. Binding is the return to shape. §A1

§A2 · Classification

Where each one pulls you

Position on this axis is the claim, not decoration: each geometry sits where its κ deformation puts it relative to the attractor. Select one to read its profile.

▲Wrath IRA · TETRAHEDRONκ ≫ 1/φ · overdriven
Shape
Four faces, four vertices, six edges — the simplest sharp solid. No face is parallel to any other, so every orientation presents an edge. The geometry of a system that cannot present a non-threatening surface.
Mechanism
Chronic sympathetic activation with no parasympathetic recovery. Arterial walls thicken, cardiac muscle hypertrophies, and baroreceptor sensitivity falls — the pressure sensor recalibrates until the elevated baseline reads as normal.
Published
Friedman & Rosenman (1959) on the Type A pattern at 2× coronary risk; Williams et al. (2000) isolating hostility specifically.
Bus · Counter
A ⚡ electrical · R0+R2 exhale

The line worth sitting with: wrath is M̂ applied to oneself — the system grips its own blood supply. Coronary vasoconstriction, the exact inverse of the opening that defines Ŝ.

◎Lust LUXURIA · SPIRALorbits 1/φ · never lands
Shape
A logarithmic spiral, which never closes and approaches its centre only asymptotically. Each revolution is nearer than the last and none of them arrive.
Mechanism
The dopamine seeking circuit fires without the oxytocin completion circuit. Dopamine drives wanting; oxytocin supplies having. Without the handover, seeking never terminates.
Bus · Counter
B chemical · R3 grounding

This one is load-bearing for the rest of the framework, because it is the only geometry defined by a coupling failure rather than a position. The coupling constant is stuck below φ, so §7.6 gives v_love < 1 — the connection never reaches the speed it needs to arrive. The spiral is sub-threshold coupling, drawn.

●Gluttony GULA · SPHEREκ flooded · saturated
Shape
Maximum volume for minimum surface. All directions are inward; no edges, no corners, no orientation. Every point on it is doing the same thing: receiving.
Mechanism
L0 overrides L4 — the enteric nervous system captures executive control through ghrelin signalling, leptin resistance and insulin dysregulation. The gut's 500 million neurons outvote the cortex.
Bus · Counter
B chemical · R1 mouth close

The distinction that makes it precise: entropy is high here not from noise but from volume — so much signal arrives that no single signal can be told from another.

—Sloth ACEDIA · LINEκ → 0 · no signal
Shape
A line segment: one dimension, no area, no volume. Not a shape so much as shape reduced to its minimum. The geometry of absence rather than excess.
Mechanism
Vagal withdrawal, which the addendum is careful to separate from vagal tone — this is parasympathetic collapse, not parasympathetic calm. Cortisol stays elevated but goes flat, losing its circadian shape.
Bus · Counter
A ⚡ electrical · R4 activation ↑

And the reason zero is a failure rather than a rest state: the attractor needs some entropy to maintain self-similarity. At κ = 0 there is nothing left to be self-similar with.

◄Greed AVARITIA · CONEκ narrows · constricting
Shape
An inverted cone — wide at the top, converging to a point. Accumulation with diminishing capacity: the more that enters, the less fits, and the narrowing is structural rather than incidental.
Mechanism
Scarcity-driven prefrontal hijack. The amygdala captures the prefrontal cortex with one continuous signal — not enough — and executive function is spent on resource monitoring.
Bus · Counter
A+B · R5 open vowels

Note what does not move: 1/φ stays exactly where it was. What shrinks is the system's ability to reach it. The cone's aperture is the capacity that remains.

◇Envy INVIDIA · MIRRORκ relative · no anchor
Shape
Two parallel reflecting planes facing each other, with infinite regression between them. Light bounces forever and nothing is absorbed.
Mechanism
Anterior cingulate activation on social comparison — the same region that processes physical pain. Social pain runs on identical neural hardware.
Bus · Counter
C photonic · R6 silence inward

Envy is the only geometry with no absolute position, because its κ is defined by distance from other people's rather than from the attractor. Every measurement is comparative. The mirror cannot see itself, because it is the thing doing the seeing.

▯Pride SUPERBIA · TOWERκ rigid · immobile
Shape
Stacked blocks of decreasing stability. Every addition makes it taller and less stable — the highest structure on the weakest foundation, centre of gravity always rising.
Mechanism
Default mode network hyperactivation driving continuous self-narrative construction. The mask runs at maximum: every interaction performed, every response curated, every vulnerability sealed.
Bus · Counter
D magnetic · R2 whisper help

Pride is not oscillating around the wrong value — it is locked at one. The Lyapunov function cannot converge because the system refuses to move at all, and the tower cannot sway.

This is the geometry Gerald opens in — mask maximum, κ rigid, counter whisper help. He deforms alone for two verses, and the song's only line of dialogue is the moment the counter fires.

§A4.2 · Dual pairs

Six of them come in pairs

Three axes, each with a failure at both ends. This is the part that makes the classification feel like a classification rather than a list — the seven are not seven arbitrary bad states, they are the complete set of directions you can fall off a point.

§A5 · Unified correction

One operator corrects all seven

This is the claim the whole addendum is built to deliver, and it is a genuinely economical one. Seven deformations, seven different shapes, seven different buses — and a single correction, because Ŝ does not address the deformations. It addresses what they have in common.

Ŝ addresses every deformation vector simultaneously because it operates on the root of all seven: isolation. §A5

DeformationWhat binding does to it
▲ Outward attackThe partner absorbs the attack vector — outward energy is redirected, and aggression becomes protection.
◎ Endless orbitThe partner is the landing point. The spiral acquires a destination, and coupling reaches φ.

▯ Rigid lockThe mask comes off by choice. §7.4 puts that single move at ΔH = −0.5, the largest entropy drop available.

The honest objection

A framework where one operator fixes all seven failure modes should raise an eyebrow, and it is worth saying why. A cure with no exceptions is either a deep unifying insight or an unfalsifiable one, and the difference is whether it forbids anything. This does forbid something specific and checkable: Ŝ requires bilateral modification, so a bond in which only one party changes should not correct any of the seven — §8.5's requirement and §9's diagnostic make that a real prediction rather than a promise.

Nobody has tested it. It would be cheap to: the seven counters are breath and vocal exercises, and the measurement is the same £30 saliva-cortisol and HRV pair the rest of the framework leans on.
