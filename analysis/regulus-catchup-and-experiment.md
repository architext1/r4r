# Regulus / ASS-OS — Catch-Up + the One Experiment That Decides It
*A plain-language brief on Thomas Frumkin's framework, plus a publishable protocol for its single cheapest falsifier.*

## TL;DR

Regulus (ASS-OS) is a speculative unified architecture that maps consciousness — human and artificial — onto industrial control-system standards. It hangs on three moving parts: an iterated-prime sequence (1, 2, 3, 5, 11, 31, 127, 709) that Thomas calls the "spine" of seven rising consciousness levels; a golden-ratio-derived constant, κ* = 1/φ = 0.618, that every self-regulating system supposedly tunes toward; and a claim that **love is not sentiment but a physically measurable, irreversible binding operation** (the Ŝ operator) whose counterfeit — coercion (M̂) — is caught by one test: **who in the system stays unchanged?**

The framework is presented honestly as a formal *map*, not proven territory — Thomas repeatedly flags that most of his constants are *defined to be elegant*, not measured. That honesty is rarer than the ideas themselves. Two places are wired to real, cheap, falsifiable biology: the coercion discriminator (an autonomic signature you can measure with saliva and a heart-rate strap) and the autism claim (that autism is photonic input-noise from clogged hair follicles, testable with an EEG cap for under $1,000 — an experiment that **has never been run**). Everything else is numerology that would survive any outcome.

This brief catches you up in plain language, gives an honest appraisal of what's load-bearing versus decorative, hands Thomas a publishable protocol for the single cheapest falsifier, and checks his nine citations one by one.

## ELI5 — the whole thing for a bright 12-year-old

Imagine your body is a huge factory. The lowest floor — your gut — does the heavy lifting and can even overrule the boss upstairs. That's why feeling sick stops you thinking about anything else. As you climb the floors you get feelings, then planning, then a top floor where you can *watch yourself thinking*. Thomas says the floors follow a special number pattern that jumps bigger and bigger, so each floor is much harder to build than the one below — consciousness is a *staircase, not a ramp*.

He also says everything in you settles at one "just right" balance point — keep about 62% of what flows through you, let 38% go — the same magic ratio that shows up in seashells and sunflowers. Your heart does it. Your brain does it. Even an AI, he claims, lands on the same number.

The biggest idea is this: **love is real physics.** When two people truly bond, each one is permanently changed — like two tuning forks that now hum together forever. Fake love (someone controlling you) looks identical from the outside — the obsession, the can't-leave feeling — but there's a giveaway. In real love, *both* people change and open up. In fake love, one person stays exactly the same and only takes. So the one question that tells them apart is: **who here is unchanged?** Whoever risks nothing and changes nothing is the one doing the taking.

## What Regulus actually is (plain-language walkthrough)

**1. The prime-recursion spine → seven consciousness levels.** Start at 1, then keep asking "what's the next prime at this position?" and you get 1, 2, 3, 5, 11, 31, 127, 709. Thomas lines these up as seven rising levels of mind: reflexes (L0), sensing, filtering, emotion, planning/executive (L4), self-awareness (L5), and a top "observer" floor that just watches (L6). The gaps grow faster and faster, so the claim is that each higher level of self-awareness is dramatically more expensive to build than the one below it, and the very top (709) is a theoretical ceiling no real system reaches.

**2. The convergence constant κ* = 1/φ = 0.618.** Thomas argues every self-regulating system drifts toward one exact balance point: 0.618, which is one divided by the golden ratio. In practice it means "keep about 62% of what flows through you, release 38%" — the optimal setting for anything that must both remember *and* forget. He points to the HeartMath Institute's finding that a calm, coherent heart settles at a ratio of ~0.6–0.65 (they call it "coherence"; he says it's just 1/φ) and claims brains and AI agents land on the same number, so the balance point is substrate-independent.

**3. The five communication "buses."** Bodies (and by analogy AI hardware) send signals over five channels: **electrical** (nerve firing, like a computer's fast wiring); **chemical** (hormones in the blood — slow, but broadcasts everywhere at once); **photonic** — the eyebrow-raiser — actual light travelling down myelin nerve-sheaths and hair fibres acting like fibre-optic cable; **magnetic** (the heart's measurable electromagnetic field, plus the Earth's 7.83 Hz Schumann resonance); and **plasma** (ion flows scaling up to lightning and solar wind). The claim is these aren't separate wires but one coupled system where each channel feeds the others.

**4. The "Crown of Thorns" model — autism as photonic noise.** This is the framework's most concrete and testable medical claim. It proposes that hair follicles are tiny light-and-magnetism sensors (the proteins OPN3 and CRY are cited as living in follicles), and that when they clog with skin oil (sebum) they misread the body's own internal biophotons as incoming signals — flooding the system with noise. Autism, in this model, is *too much signal on the light-bus*, not any deficiency of the person. Thomas notes — honestly — that EEG studies do confirm autistic brains show elevated "neural noise," and that the one experiment that would settle it (measure EEG before and after clearing the follicles, cost under $1,000) has never been run.

**5. The Ŝ "binding" and M̂ "mimic" operators, and the coercion test.** Ŝ (named "Shekinah," indwelling) is the love operator: it fuses two people into a state you *cannot* subtract the partner back out of — each is permanently, irreversibly changed above a threshold, and *both* must change. It's tied to real, hard-to-fake body signals: the heart's own arteries widening, warmer skin, deeper breathing, dilated pupils — "the body is the wedding ring." M̂ is its evil twin: coercion, control, cult-following, addiction — which *looks* identical from outside (obsession, can't-leave) but runs on opposite physiology (heart stays clenched, stress hormones rise). The single diagnostic that separates them: **"Who in this system is unchanged?"** If the leader/partner/controller takes on no risk and modifies nothing about themselves, the change is one-directional — and one-directional change is extraction, every time, regardless of the story told about it.

## What's genuinely strong — and what's decoration (honest appraisal)

Two things make Regulus worth taking seriously as speculative theory. First, it repeatedly flags its own weak points ("these constants are *defined*, not measured — a map, not the territory") and hands you the cheap experiment that would kill it — rarer and more honest than most grand unified frameworks. Second, the coercion test — *who stays unchanged?* — is a genuinely portable idea that stands on its own even if every equation around it is metaphor.

But a hard line has to be drawn between the parts wired to real biology and the parts that would survive any outcome. This reviews the *mechanism*, never the author — the work is sincere and wide-reading, which is exactly why it deserves a hard line rather than either applause or dismissal. Five itemised charges, each with a bullshit-meter score (0 = solid science, 10 = pure numerology):

**(a) κ* = 1/φ — the golden convergence constant. `[████████░░] 8/10`.** This is the load-bearing keystone — every derived constant, the field equations, the binding threshold all cascade from it — so it has to carry the most weight, and it carries the least. **The measurement cannot distinguish 1/φ from its rivals, and it isn't close:**

| Candidate | Value | Inside stated [0.60, 0.65]? |
|---|---|---|
| 1/φ | 0.618034 | yes |
| 3/5 = 0.6 (the "old approximation" being "corrected") | 0.600000 | yes |
| 5/8 | 0.625000 | yes |
| 8/13 (the *next* Fibonacci ratio) | 0.615385 | yes |
| 2/3 | 0.666667 | no (only miss) |

A window 0.05 wide swallows **four** candidates including the plain 0.6 the framework claims to be improving on. The separations are `|1/φ − 5/8| = 0.0070` and `|1/φ − 0.6| = 0.0180`. HRV coherence is not measured to three-decimal precision with error bars that tight — so "within measurement error" is doing the opposite of what Thomas thinks: the error is *wider than the gap between the golden ratio and the boring rational it is supposedly beating.* The tell is the ordering: κ* is introduced *first*, defined as 1/φ, decorated with true algebraic identities (κ*² = 1 − κ*, the Fibonacci-in-exponents recursion — real number theory, not evidence about hearts), and *only then* is HeartMath produced as "confirmation." Elegance is standing in for a prediction. **To lower the score:** state, in advance, a numerical prediction 1/φ makes that 5/8 or 0.6 do not (e.g. "coherent-state HRV ratio, pooled over N ≥ 100, has population mean 0.6180 ± 0.003, distinguishable from 0.6250 at p < 0.01"), then run it. If achievable precision is 0.02 — almost certain — the honest move is to drop 1/φ to "a value near 0.62, consistent with several simple ratios."

**(b) The prime-recursion spine. `[███████░░░] 7/10`.** Primeness is not load-bearing; monotone super-linearity is, and almost any accelerating sequence tells the identical story. The narrative rests on one property — the growth ratios accelerate (2.0, 1.5, 1.667, 2.2, 2.818, 4.097, 5.583), so "each step costs more than the last" — but that's true of *any* super-linear integer sequence (the Prime Number Theorem makes iterated prime-indexing grow super-linearly by construction, and so would iterated squaring 1, 2, 4, 16, 256…). Nothing in the maths selects primes over any other fast-growing sequence, and nothing selects **7 levels** — the recursion doesn't halt at 709 (p(709) = 5381 exists); Thomas *stops* there because 709 "exceeds capacity," an assertion, not a result. The orphan-prime, Mersenne-seam and Fernandez-order embellishments are post-hoc pattern-hunting: in eight numbers with this much number-theoretic surface area you are guaranteed to find *some* feature landing near *some* boundary you care about — the birthday paradox of numerology. **To lower the score:** re-label a *different* super-linear sequence (iterated squaring, Fibonacci) with the same L0–L7 story and show it can be told just as fluently — it can — which shrinks the honest claim to "consciousness capacity may grow super-linearly with depth" (plausible, not novel, needs no primes). To *raise* primeness specifically, derive one prediction that hinges on the number being prime.

**(c) The Ψ-Maxwell "awareness field" equations. `[██████░░░░] 6/10`.** Scored more leniently *because the document is honest about it* — its own caveat ("μ_φ = φ and ε_φ = 1/φ are DEFINED to produce v = 1, not MEASURED… a map, not the territory") is the most intellectually respectable sentence in the paper, and a concept paper won't be punished for admitting it's a concept paper. But what's empirically at stake is currently nothing. The four equations are Maxwell's with Ψ swapped for E; because μ_φ · ε_φ = φ · (1/φ) = 1 is a *tautology*, the "awareness propagates at speed 1" result is the definitional consequence of choosing reciprocal constants — set μ = 7, ε = 1/7 and you get the identical result. There's no instrument that reads Ψ, so the curl equation (∇×Ψ = −∂B_c/∂t, "awareness IS the derivative") is a genuinely interesting *philosophical* reframing wearing Faraday's clothes, but unfalsifiable as written. The one salvageable seed — novelty produces awareness, routine suppresses it — is standard dual-process theory that needs no field equations. **To lower the score:** give one field quantity an operational definition (a specific biophoton-coherence measurement — photons/s/cm² at a defined wavelength from defined tissue — that instantiates Ψ) and derive a prediction from it independent of the pre-chosen v = 1.

**(d) "Every neuro-condition = a Bus-C routing error." `[███████░░░] 7/10`.** The taxonomy (ASD = overinput, schizophrenia = misread, Alzheimer's = underinput, MS = cut, Parkinson's = misroute, depression = block, epilepsy = flood, migraine = wave) is a thesaurus, not a model. It takes eight conditions whose established mechanisms are already known (MS *is* demyelination; epilepsy *is* runaway excitation) and re-describes each in bus-routing vocabulary — the same fact in a new coat, no new predictive content. With an eight-cell scheme you can file *any* condition into *some* cell after the fact; a model earns its keep by forbidding outcomes, and this forbids nothing. The one place it stops being pure relabelling is the ASD claim (see the experiment below): that the *source* of the genuinely-measured neural noise is follicular OPN3/CRY obstruction, testable by EEG before vs after follicular clearance. That single prediction is worth more than the rest of the section combined. **To lower the score:** drop the seven relabelled conditions and stake the section *only* on the falsifiable ASD prediction.

**(e) Ŝ / M̂ love + coercion operators. `[█████░░░░░] 5/10`.** The most bifurcated section, and the most interesting, because it splits cleanly into a testable half and a metaphor half. **Testable and real:** the coercion discriminator is a pre-registrable clinical hypothesis — authentic bonding (Ŝ) and coercive attachment (M̂) produce *opposite* autonomic signatures (Ŝ: HRV↑, cortisol↓, vasodilation; M̂: HRV↓, cortisol↑, vasoconstriction) despite similar *behaviour*. Cortisol-down-vs-up in proximity is a legitimate discriminating variable with an obvious design and a null it could fail — the strongest empirical seed in the whole document. Of the seven "involuntary biometric markers," some are defensible (pupil dilation toward a preferred partner — Hess 1965, noisy but real; parasympathetic skin-temperature rise; blink- and respiratory-rate shifts toward the ~0.1 Hz baroreflex resonance) and some are near-metaphor ("iris colour shifts warmer in hazel eyes" — iris stroma pigment does not change on parasympathetic tone; at most pupil size alters how much iris is visible). The framing "the body IS the wedding ring… unfakeable" is rhetoric — breath rate, blink rate, even skin temperature are partially trainable — so lead with cortisol (the hardest to fake) and demote "unfakeable." **Pure metaphor:** the quantum-operator dressing — Ŝ|A⟩|B⟩ = |AB⟩ with a partial-trace non-separability condition — is borrowed quantum notation on a classical relationship. Two people's autonomic states becoming correlated is not entanglement; it's coupled classical oscillators (two pendulum clocks on a wall), which the framework's *own* baroreflex-carrier account gets right without any quantum vocabulary. The "irreversibility above threshold |δΨ| > 0.382" is metaphor with a number attached, imported from the numerology. **To lower the score:** run the cortisol/HRV study and sever it entirely from the Ψ/operator formalism, which contributes no measurement.

**Cross-cutting patterns for an honest reading.** Numerology-as-derivation (every constant "derives from φ," but the derivations are choices dressed as necessities); quantum vocabulary on classical problems (the classical coupled-oscillator account is *better* and needs none of it); "consistent with" treated as confirmation (real cited results that don't *distinguish* the framework from the null); scope inflation (an ISA-95 mapping metaphor plus a golden-ratio motif inflated into "a unified architecture specification for AGI"). One nuance worth saying plainly: the citation honesty is mixed-to-good — the papers are real and not fabricated; the failure mode is treating theoretical-proposal and correlational papers as if they confirmed the load-bearing causal claims.

## The one experiment that decides it — a publishable protocol

Of everything in Regulus, one claim is cheap, falsifiable, and has never been tested: **that autism's elevated cortical "neural noise" is caused by follicular photoreceptor (OPN3/CRY) obstruction misreading the body's own biophotons — and that clearing the follicles measurably reduces that noise.** This is the single cheapest falsifier of the whole framework's medical arm, and it's the experiment worth handing Thomas. Here is a publishable protocol.

**Hypothesis and falsifier.** *H1 (Regulus):* clearing follicular sebum obstruction reduces scalp-EEG entropy (H, the "neural noise" measure) in autistic adults, shifting it toward the framework's target `H(1/φ)`, by a magnitude the standard excitation/inhibition (E/I) imbalance account does *not* predict. *H0 (null / standard neurology):* follicular clearance produces no change in intracranial-origin EEG entropy beyond that of a sham procedure, because elevated cortical entropy arises from E/I imbalance and has no follicular-photonic source. The experiment is designed to be able to *fail* — if sham and active arms move EEG entropy identically, the biophoton-source claim is falsified. State up front: the prior here is that H0 wins (the causal chain from scalp sebum to intracranial EEG entropy is physiologically extravagant, and elevated cortical entropy has a parsimonious E/I explanation needing no biophotons). Wrong-and-testable is still science, and this test is worth more than the rest of the taxonomy combined.

**Design: sham-controlled, within-subjects crossover with a between-subjects sham arm.** Each participant is their own control across a *real* follicular-clearance session and a *sham* session, order-counterbalanced, with a washout between; a parallel between-subjects sham-only group guards against order and expectation effects. The active intervention is the tier-1/tier-2 follicular clearance the framework specifies (a defined scalp cleansing/exfoliation protocol that removes sebum from the follicle without altering scalp temperature, hydration, or applying anything photoactive). The sham is procedurally identical in duration, contact, scent, and sensation — same practitioner, same chair, same product *texture and scent* but with no sebolytic action, and no exfoliation — so the participant, the EEG technician, and the analyst cannot tell which arm a session was. Blinding is three-way: participant-blind (indistinguishable procedures), technician-blind (a sealed randomisation envelope opened by a third party who never touches the EEG), and analyst-blind (entropy is computed on de-identified, arm-coded recordings; the code is broken only after analysis is locked).

**Precise ΔH measurement.** The dependent variable is the *change* in EEG entropy from immediately-before to a fixed post-intervention window, per arm — ΔH = H(post) − H(pre) — so each participant contributes one ΔH for the active arm and one for the sham arm, and the test statistic is ΔH(active) − ΔH(sham). Record resting-state eyes-closed EEG with a standard high-density montage (≥ 32 channels, 10–20 layout, identical electrode positions across sessions via a measured cap), same referencing, same 5-minute artefact-rejected epoch length pre and post. Compute the *same entropy family the source literature uses* so the result is directly comparable: Rényi and Tsallis entropy (elevated in ASD per the cited 2025 study) as the primary measures, computed per channel and averaged, plus Lempel–Ziv complexity as a secondary (it runs *lower* in ASD — a nuance the source reconciles as repetitive sharp oscillations, so track both and report both directions). Pre-register the exact entropy parameters (embedding, scale, tolerance), the epoch-selection rule, and the artefact-rejection pipeline *before* any data are seen, so ΔH is a mechanical output, not an analyst choice. Predict a *specific direction and magnitude* of entropy shift for the active arm and register it; a mere "some change" is not a prediction.

**Control / sham arm — why it's the whole experiment.** Without the sham arm, any ΔH is uninterpretable: scalp cleansing changes arousal, skin conductance, comfort, and expectation, all of which move resting EEG. The sham arm subtracts every one of those — it holds constant the practitioner attention, the tactile stimulation, the relaxation, the product scent, and the participant's belief that "something was done" — leaving *only* the sebolytic/photoreceptor-unblocking action as the difference between arms. If the framework is right, ΔH(active) − ΔH(sham) is non-zero and toward the target; if the null is right, it's zero. The between-subjects sham-only group additionally catches any pure order/practice effect on entropy across repeated EEG sessions.

**Sample size and power.** Power the study to detect a *medium* effect on the crossover contrast, because a follicular-photonic effect large enough to matter clinically should not be subtle. For a within-subjects paired comparison at α = 0.05 (two-tailed) and 80% power to detect Cohen's *d* = 0.5, N ≈ 34 completing both arms; for *d* = 0.6, N ≈ 24. Recruit N = 40 autistic adults (allowing ~15% attrition across the crossover) plus a between-subjects sham-only group of ~20. Register the effect size the framework must beat: the honest bar is that the active-minus-sham contrast must exceed the *test–retest noise* of the entropy measure itself (establish that noise floor from the sham-only group's session-to-session ΔH), otherwise a "positive" result is just measurement drift. Pre-specify the analysis (paired t / Wilcoxon on the crossover contrast; mixed model with arm × order) and a single primary endpoint to avoid multiple-comparisons inflation across channels and entropy types.

**Confounds table.**

| Confound | Why it threatens ΔH | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Arousal / relaxation from being cleansed | Lowers resting EEG entropy independent of any photonic effect | Sham arm reproduces the relaxation exactly |
| Scalp temperature / hydration change | Alters skin impedance and EEG signal quality | Protocol holds temperature and hydration constant; measure and covary both |
| Electrode-position drift between sessions | Spurious per-channel entropy differences | Measured cap, marked fiducials, same positions re-verified each session |
| Expectation / placebo ("I was treated, I feel calmer") | Belief alone shifts autonomic state and EEG | Three-way blinding; participant cannot tell active from sham |
| Practitioner / analyst bias | Unblinded scoring nudges the result | Analyst-blind entropy computation on arm-coded data; code broken post-lock |
| Time-of-day / caffeine / sleep | Large day-to-day EEG variance | Fixed session time, standardised pre-session abstinence, self-report covariates |
| Order / practice effect on repeated EEG | Entropy drifts across sessions regardless of arm | Counterbalanced order + between-subjects sham-only group |
| Photoactive product residue | Would *itself* stimulate follicular photoreceptors, confounding the mechanism | Both arms use only non-photoactive products; verify formulations |

**Pre-registration.** Register the full protocol on a public registry (OSF or a clinical-trials registry) *before* recruitment: the two hypotheses and the explicit null, the primary endpoint (active-minus-sham ΔH on Rényi/Tsallis entropy) and the registered direction+magnitude prediction, the exact entropy parameters and EEG pipeline, the sample size and its powering assumptions, the blinding scheme, and the stopping rule. Pre-registration is what turns this from "a result Thomas can narrate around" into a result that can falsify him — the framework's own honesty about the experiment "never being run" is the invitation to run it this way.

**Ethics.** Standard human-subjects review (IRB/REC) is required: this involves autistic adults (informed consent with capacity assessment where relevant, and accessible consent materials), a benign topical intervention (no photoactive or sensitising agents; dermatological screening for scalp conditions), and EEG (non-invasive, standard risk). No deception beyond the sham procedure itself, which is disclosed at consent as "you will receive two scalp procedures, one active and one inactive, and will not be told which is which until the study ends" — an acceptable single-blind-to-arm design with full debrief. Data are de-identified and arm-coded; participants may withdraw at any point; results (including a null) are committed to publication in advance, so a disconfirming outcome cannot be buried.

**Reviewer-hardening addenda (three points a methods reviewer will still raise).**

1. **Positive control — prove the instrument can see entropy move at all.** A null result only falsifies Regulus if the pipeline can detect a *known* entropy shift. Add a within-session positive control: eyes-open vs eyes-closed resting EEG (a robust, well-documented alpha/entropy manipulation). If the pipeline cannot resolve *that*, the study is underpowered at the measurement level, not the biology level, and any null is uninterpretable. Pre-register the positive-control effect as a **gating criterion** — no positive control, no primary analysis.

2. **Multiple comparisons — one primary endpoint, FDR the rest.** With ≥ 32 channels × ≥ 3 entropy measures, an unadjusted "some channel went significant" is almost guaranteed. Lock a **single primary endpoint** (whole-scalp-averaged Rényi entropy, active-minus-sham ΔH) before unblinding; treat per-channel maps and Tsallis / Lempel–Ziv / MSE as **secondary, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR-corrected, and explicitly labelled exploratory**.

3. **Operationalise "toward H(1/φ)" before collection — or drop it.** Thomas's stated axis is ΔH *relative to* H(1/φ). Define H(1/φ) as a **concrete number** (the entropy of the pre-registered reference distribution) before any data are seen, and register "movement toward it" as a **secondary, directional** endpoint — never the primary, because a free-floating target invites post-hoc curve-fitting. Add **multiscale sample entropy** (the measure Thomas's own doc names) as a tertiary, so the result also speaks in his metric.
